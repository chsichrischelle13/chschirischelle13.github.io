GoHighLevel System Overview

CoolAir — Lead Qualification & Consultation Booking Automation

A self-initiated portfolio system for a fictional home-service business. The architecture is designed around a single inquiry journey: capture → qualify → follow up → hand off or book → track appointment outcome.

CA 02Consultation Follow-Up

CA 04Consultation Booked

The project uses fictional test contacts and is presented as a portfolio demonstration rather than a production client system.

Validation & Test Results

Evidence from workflow execution logs.

The captured GoHighLevel execution logs provide validation evidence for the major paths in the CoolAir system. Results below describe what is visible in the recorded test runs.

CA 01 — Inquiry Intake Executed / FinishedTest log shows the inquiry entering the workflow, being tagged and assigned, an opportunity being found/created, the service-area branch running, the opportunity being updated, an email being sent, and the contact reaching the end of the workflow.
CA 02 — Consultation Follow-Up Executed / FinishedCaptured run shows the follow-up path reaching a “Showed” outcome, updating the opportunity, creating the estimate-preparation task, and then ending the workflow.
CA 03 — Customer Reply Handoff ExecutedCaptured run shows the reply branch, internal notification, staff task, opportunity update, and workflow removal. One removal action was still displayed as “Processing” in the screenshot at capture time.
CA 04 — Consultation Booked Executed / FinishedCaptured run shows opportunity creation, booking tags, appointment notification, and workflow completion after the consultation was booked.
CA 05 — Consultation Outcome Executed / FinishedCaptured runs show both cancellation and “showed” outcome paths, including opportunity updates, outcome-specific actions/notifications, and removal at the end of the workflow.
Validation approachTesting used fictional contacts and execution-log review. The portfolio presents observed workflow behavior rather than claiming client revenue, conversion, or time-savings results.

Validation evidence is based on the execution-log screenshots captured during testing on September 26, 2026.
